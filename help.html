🆘 Cyber Safety Help & Tips

🔍 How to identify a phishing link?

  • Look for strange domains (e.g., free-gift.amazon.co.in.xyz)
  • Check spelling errors and random characters
  • Hover over links to see the real URL

⚠ I clicked on a suspicious link! What should I do?

  • Immediately disconnect from internet if something downloads
  • Do not enter any personal information
  • Scan your device using antivirus (even offline ones)

🛡 How to stay safe online?

  • Use strong, unique passwords
  • Do not click on unknown emails or WhatsApp links
  • Install software only from trusted sources
